For decades, local businesses relied on physical on-premise servers to store records and run software. Today, maintaining physical server hardware in Nigeria is increasingly complex due to depreciation, physical security vulnerabilities, and frequent power instability.

## The Advantages of Cloud Infrastructure

Migrating to modern cloud platforms (like AWS, Google Cloud, or Vercel) offers distinct operational benefits:
- **Reduced Overhead**: Eliminate the upfront capital costs of buying servers, maintaining dedicated server closets, and running continuous generator cooling.
- **Protection from Outages**: Physical power failures no longer threaten your data integrity when paired with architectures that prevent [data loss during frequent power outages](/blog/preventing-data-loss-in-power-outages).
- **Automatic Scalability**: Scale system resources up or down automatically based on user traffic spikes without buying new boxes.
- **Robust Disaster Recovery**: Automated cloud backups ensure business continuity even in the event of local office emergencies or hardware failure.

If your regulatory environment or local compliance mandates keeping sensitive records on-site, you can adopt a [hybrid cloud infrastructure](/blog/hybrid-cloud-infrastructure) that marries local data sovereignty with cloud speed.
