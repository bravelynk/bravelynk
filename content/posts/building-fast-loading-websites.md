If your company website takes more than three seconds to load on a smartphone, nearly half of your visitors will bounce before they even see your headline or browse your products.

## Why Load Speed Dictates Revenue

- **Better Search Rankings**: Google prioritizes fast-loading, mobile-friendly websites with high Core Web Vitals, delivering compounding organic traffic.
- **Higher Conversion Rates**: Customers are far more likely to complete quotation forms and checkout flows on interfaces that respond instantaneously.
- **Lower Mobile Bandwidth**: Optimized web apps consume significantly less mobile data, which is vital for local users across Nigerian cellular networks.

Speed is not merely an engineering metric — it is a direct contributor to your bottom line.
