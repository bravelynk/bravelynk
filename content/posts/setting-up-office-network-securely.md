Your local office network is the gateway to your business data. A poorly configured Wi-Fi router or unmanaged switch leaves company workstations and internal records vulnerable to unauthorized intrusions and eavesdropping.

## Core Network Security Steps

1. **Segment Local Networks (VLANs)**: Create isolated virtual LANs or separate SSIDs for guest visitors, keeping unauthorized devices off the network where servers and accounting machines communicate.
2. **Implement Hardware Resilience**: Power routers, switches, and edge gateways through battery backup systems to [prevent data loss during frequent power outages](/blog/preventing-data-loss-in-power-outages).
3. **Disable Remote Management**: Disallow router administration from the public WAN to prevent external brute-force credential attacks.
4. **Audit Connected Endpoints**: Schedule a periodic [technology infrastructure audit](/blog/the-value-of-a-tech-audit) to identify rogue devices, unpatched firmware, and exposed ports.
