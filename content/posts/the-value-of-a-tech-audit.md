Just like a financial audit protects your balance sheet, a technology infrastructure audit evaluates the operational health, reliability, and security of your digital systems. Many businesses unknowingly operate with bottlenecks that leak revenue and leave data exposed.

## What a Professional Tech Audit Uncovers

- **Wasted Software Spend**: Unused SaaS subscriptions and duplicate licenses that inflate monthly operating expenses.
- **Single Points of Failure**: Local machines holding critical records without backups, leaving you vulnerable to [data loss during frequent power outages](/blog/preventing-data-loss-in-power-outages).
- **Network Vulnerabilities**: Open router ports and unsegmented networks that can be hardened by [setting up a secure office network](/blog/setting-up-office-network-securely).
- **Cloud Readiness**: Systems ready to benefit from [cloud migration for SMEs](/blog/cloud-migration-benefits-nigerian-smes) or a [hybrid cloud infrastructure](/blog/hybrid-cloud-infrastructure).

A professional technology audit provides an executive roadmap of what to upgrade, what to secure, and what subscriptions to eliminate.
