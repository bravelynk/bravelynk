If you are building an e-commerce store, SaaS platform, or billing app in Nigeria, selecting the right payment gateway integration is critical for client conversions and smooth financial reconciliation.

## Paystack vs. Flutterwave APIs

- **Paystack**: Renowned for exceptionally clean developer documentation, intuitive SDKs, reliable bank transfer webhooks, and seamless recurring billing. It forms the backbone of systems that [automate client invoice reminders](/blog/automating-invoice-reminders).
- **Flutterwave**: Offers expansive multi-currency settlement options, mobile money collections, and cross-border payment acceptance across multiple African markets.
- **Transaction Fees & Reliability**: Both gateways offer comparable local processing charges (typically 1.5% with fee caps) and high uptime for card transactions.

When implementing payment flows into client-facing platforms, security is non-negotiable. Developers should review standards for [mobile banking app security](/blog/mobile-banking-security-best-practices) and ensure proper webhook signature verification using [robust API integrations](/blog/understanding-api-integrations).
