Customer support is vital for retention, but managing customer requests through fragmented WhatsApp messages and shared inboxes leads to delayed responses, missed follow-ups, and frustrated clients.

## Implementing Automated Support Pipelines

- **Unified Inboxes**: Aggregate customer inquiries from web forms, email, and WhatsApp into a centralized queue through [custom API integrations](/blog/understanding-api-integrations).
- **Intelligent Routing**: Automatically route technical queries to developers and billing questions to accounting, eliminating manual triaging.
- **Automated Acknowledgment**: Send immediate confirmation notices with expected resolution times so clients know their request is being handled.
- **Self-Service Portals**: Implement knowledge bases and client portals as part of a strategy for [automating 70% of routine operations](/blog/why-automation-is-your-real-roi).

For organizations with specialized workflows, moving beyond off-the-shelf tools into [custom ERP and CRM systems](/blog/custom-erp-vs-ready-made) can unlock massive operational savings.
